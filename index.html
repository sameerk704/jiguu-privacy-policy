Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

JIGUU – Learn Smart. Study Smarter. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the JIGUU mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information and how we protect the privacy of our users.

1. About the App

JIGUU is an educational application designed to support students from Classes 7 to 12. The app provides NCERT-based educational resources, solutions, study materials, formulas, quizzes, practice content, and other learning resources to help students learn effectively.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from users through the JIGUU application. The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to access its educational content.

3. Permissions

The app may request internet permission when required to load or update educational content and other online resources. JIGUU is designed to provide educational content with offline access where available. We do not use these permissions to collect, store, or share personally identifiable information.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising services for collecting or tracking users' personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

JIGUU is an educational application intended for students, including school students who may be under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children or other users through the app.

6. Data Security

We value the privacy of our users. Since JIGUU does not collect or require personally identifiable information for its core educational functionality, there is no personal user information maintained by us for such purposes. We take reasonable measures to maintain the privacy and security of the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, its features, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page, along with an updated "Last Updated" date.
